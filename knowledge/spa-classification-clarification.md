# Spa classification clarification

Source: original GMB Outbound Playbook, pages 1, 2 and 4. This is a derived reading aid; the PDF Who/Skip rows and fixed templates remain the source of truth.

PDF-grounded Spa classification clarification (pages 1, 2 and 4):
- Page 1 explicitly includes "massage spa" as a GMB search term alongside "day spa", "facial spa" and "skin care studio". Massage services or a massage-focused search result alone are not a Skip reason.
- Search terms discover candidates; they do not prove qualification. The Who row still requires an independent day spa, owner-run, one location, 3+ staff, 50+ reviews and 2+ years open. Establish that the business is an independent day spa from its profile/menu/business evidence; a massage parlor, massage clinic or bathhouse label alone neither proves nor disproves that.
- Facials are not a mandatory Who criterion. Page 1 allows a hook naming a staff member OR facial; page 4 allows a named staff member OR service. Spa Jardin being facials-led on page 2 is a worked example, not an additional universal eligibility rule. Keep existing fixed templates and safe named-staff hook checks.
- The Skip row remains exact: listings of Botox, filler, med spa or aesthetics, plus chains and franchises. Do not add massage-only, no-facials, sauna, bathhouse or no-skin-care exclusions that the PDF does not state.
- Do not approve a candidate merely because massage spa is an allowed search. Missing evidence means unverified, not qualified. If the available profile/menu cannot establish the independent-day-spa requirement, retain that uncertainty and ask VJ to resolve the boundary rather than invent a new ICP or rule.
